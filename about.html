Our story

From a hardware counter to a machine shop.

Udhyog Traders started in 2009 in a rented shop in Vatva with two people, a stock of fasteners and a list of local engineering units. The business was straightforward: supply good hardware, on time, with the right paperwork.

Within a few years customers were sending drawings along with their fastener orders. They wanted brackets machined, guards formed, sprockets matched to worn samples — and they did not want to add another vendor to manage. We bought our first CNC lathe in 2014 to answer that.

Today the plant runs six lathes, three machining centres and a fabrication bay, alongside a trading desk that still stocks the fasteners we began with. The mix is deliberate. Manufacturing gives us control over quality; trading lets us be the single window our customers asked for.

We remain a family-run business. Decisions on price, priority and delivery are made by people you can call directly.

How we operate

Three rules we do not bend.

Say what is true

If a tolerance is beyond our equipment, we say so at quotation stage. A politely declined enquiry costs you a day; a bad batch costs you a production week.

Respect the date

Delivery dates are set from real machine loading. When something slips, you hear it from us before the date passes, with a revised plan attached.

Keep the paperwork

Drawings, revision levels, heat numbers and inspection records are filed per part. Ask for the certificate on a 2019 batch and we can still find it.
