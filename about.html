Our story

From a stationery counter to a corrugation line.

Udhyog Traders started in 2009 in a rented shop in Vatva with two people, a stock of kraft paper and a list of local shops and converters. The business was straightforward: supply good paper, on time, at a price that left everyone room to trade.

Within a few years customers were asking for the finished article — cartons in their own size, bags with their logo, containers for a takeaway counter. They did not want to add three vendors to manage. We installed our first corrugator in 2014 to answer that.

Today the plant runs two corrugators, printing and box making, three bag machines and a separate food-grade area for bagasse and areca tableware. The mix is deliberate. Manufacturing gives us control over strength, print and food contact; trading lets us be the single window our customers asked for.

We remain a family-run business. Decisions on price, priority and delivery are made by people you can call directly.

How we operate

Three rules we do not bend.

Say what is true

If a board grade will not hold your load, or a food-contact paper is not available, we say so at quotation stage. A declined enquiry costs you a day; a failed lot costs you a season.

Respect the date

Delivery dates are set from real line loading. When something slips, you hear it from us before the date passes, with a revised plan attached.

Keep the paperwork

Dielines, revision levels, lot numbers and test results are filed per SKU. Ask for the declaration on a 2021 batch and we can still find it.
