Who we are

One partner for the parts you make and the parts you buy.

We began in 2009 as a trading house supplying fasteners and steel to engineering units around Ahmedabad. Our customers kept asking for the same thing: someone who could also machine the components. So we built that capability — a machine shop, a fabrication bay and a quality lab under one roof.

Today Udhyog Traders manufactures to drawing and still trades in the hardware our clients need alongside it, which keeps ordering, inspection and documentation in one place.

  • Dimensional reports and material certificates with every batch
  • Drawings reviewed by our engineers before quoting, not after
  • Kanban and scheduled-release supply for repeat parts

How we work

From enquiry to dispatch, four steps.

No mystery quotes and no silent delays — you get a named contact and a date you can plan a line around.

Drawing review

Send a PDF, STEP file or a physical sample. Our engineer flags tolerances, material and finish before pricing.

Quote & sample

Itemised quotation within 2 working days, followed by a first-article sample for your approval.

Production

Scheduled runs with in-process checks at first-off, mid-batch and final inspection, logged against your part number.

Dispatch

Packed to your bin or pallet spec, with inspection report, MTC and invoice mailed together.

Quality

Paperwork that survives an audit.

A rejection at your incoming inspection costs both of us. Every batch leaves here measured, recorded and traceable to the heat number of the material it was made from.

  • First-article inspection reports with ballooned drawings for new parts
  • EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates for all traded steel and fasteners
  • Calibrated instruments — CMM, profile projector, hardness and roughness testers
  • Rejection & CAPA records maintained per customer, reviewed monthly
