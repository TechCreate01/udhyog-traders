Who we are

One supplier for what you pack in and what you serve on.

We started in 2009 as a paper and stationery trading house in Vatva, supplying kraft paper, boards and carry bags to shops and small converters around Ahmedabad. Customers kept asking for the finished article at a sensible price, in quantity, on a date they could plan around.

So we built the plant: two corrugators, printing, bag making and a food-grade area under one roof. Today we manufacture the bulk of what we sell and still trade in the films, tapes and tableware that fill the gaps.

  • Food-contact declarations and batch codes with every lot
  • Dielines and spec sheets checked before we quote, not after
  • Scheduled monthly release for repeat SKUs

How we work

From enquiry to dispatch, four steps.

No mystery pricing and no silent delays — you get a named contact and a date your packing plan can rely on.

Spec review

Send a SKU list, a sample of what you use now, or a dieline. We check size, board grade, print and food-contact needs before pricing.

Quote & sample

Itemised quotation within 2 working days, followed by a printed or moulded sample for your approval.

Production

Scheduled runs with checks on board strength, print registration and weight, logged against your SKU code.

Dispatch

Bundled and palletised to your handling spec, with batch code, test report and invoice mailed together.

Quality

Paperwork that clears your QA gate.

A failed burst strength or a missing food-contact paper costs both of us a week. Every lot leaves here tested, coded and traceable to the paper or resin batch it came from.

  • FSSAI food-contact declarations for every disposable and food-grade SKU
  • Burst strength, ECT and GSM reports from in-house testing on each paper lot
  • Batch, manufacture and expiry coding printed on cartons and labels
  • Rejection & CAPA records kept per customer and reviewed monthly
