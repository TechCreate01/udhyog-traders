Materials & standards

Specified, certified, traceable.

Paper and resin are bought against the specification, not from whatever is on the rack. Incoming lot numbers are recorded against the batch so a declaration can be produced years later.

  • Kraft & linerboard — 120–230 GSM, virgin or recycled, FSC-certified on request
  • Food-grade paper — virgin board and grease-resistant liners for direct contact
  • Moulded fibre — bagasse pulp and areca leaf, compostable and microwave-safe
  • Plastics — PP, HDPE, LDPE, BOPP and PET films; aluminium foil containers
  • Standards — IS 2771, ASTM D642 / ECT, FSSAI Schedule IV, ISTA transit
