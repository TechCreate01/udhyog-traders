Machining, fabrication and the hardware that goes with it.

Six production lines under one roof, supported by a trading desk for everything we do not make ourselves. Send a drawing, a sample or a bought-out parts list.

What we make

Production lines

Typical work is 50 to 5,000 pieces per part number, repeated monthly. Prototypes are welcome when they lead to a schedule.

CNC machined components

Turned and milled parts for assemblies where the fit matters: shafts, bushes, spacers, housings, adapter plates and sensor brackets.

  • Bar-fed turning up to Ø65 mm, chuck work to Ø350 mm
  • 3-axis milling with fixture design in-house
  • Deburring, tapping, threading and surface treatment coordinated
  • ±0.01 mm
  • SS 304 / 316L
  • Al 6061-T6
  • EN8 / EN19
  • Brass

Sheet metal fabrication

Guards, machine covers, electrical enclosures, frames, trays and mounting brackets — cut from drawing, formed, welded and finished.

  • Fibre laser cutting 0.8–12 mm mild steel, 6 mm stainless
  • CNC press braking up to 160 tonnes, 3 m bed
  • MIG and TIG welding with jig-based repeatability
  • Laser cut
  • Press brake
  • MIG / TIG
  • Powder coat
  • Zinc plating

Fasteners & pipe fittings

The trading desk carries a working stock of the standard hardware our customers reorder, plus imported grades on indent.

  • Hex bolts, studs, nuts, washers, spring and anchor fasteners
  • MS, HT (8.8 / 10.9 / 12.9), stainless and hot-dip galvanised
  • MS/SS flanges, elbows, tees, nipples, clamps and unions
  • Class 8.8
  • Class 10.9
  • SS 304
  • HDG
  • Nylon insert nuts

Castings & forgings

As-cast and proof-machined parts from foundry and forging partners we have worked with for over a decade, inspected on arrival at our plant.

  • Grey iron and SG iron castings up to 120 kg per piece
  • Forged flanges, shafts, rings and blocks
  • Heat treatment, normalising and stress relieving arranged
  • CI
  • SG iron
  • Forged flanges
  • MTC 3.1

Gears, sprockets & spares

Replacement and OEM transmission parts, matched either to your drawing or reverse-engineered from a worn sample.

  • Spur gears and simplex/duplex chain sprockets
  • Bushes, pulleys, couplings, keys and keyways
  • Hardened and ground options on request
  • Spur gears
  • Sprockets
  • Bushes
  • Couplings

Assemblies & kitting

We build the sub-assembly so your line only has to bolt it on — or bag and label hardware kits per bin for scheduled release.

  • Sub-assemblies built to a bill of materials and tested for function
  • Hardware kits bagged, labelled and barcoded per bin
  • Kanban and monthly scheduled-release supply
  • Sub-assembly
  • Kitting
  • Barcoding
  • Kanban

Materials & standards

Specified, certified, traceable.

Material is bought against the drawing, not from whatever is on the rack. Heat numbers on incoming stock are recorded against the batch so a certificate can be produced years later.

  • Carbon steel — EN8, EN9, EN19, IS 2062 plates and sections
  • Stainless steel — 304, 316L sheet, bar and pipe
  • Aluminium — 6061-T6, 6082-T6 plate and extrusion
  • Non-ferrous — brass, phosphor bronze, copper
  • Standards — IS, DIN, ASTM and customer-specific specifications
